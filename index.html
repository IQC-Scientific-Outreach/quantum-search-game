Game goals

You and your opponent both must find gems hidden among collection of worthless cards. Your opponent plays as a classical computer and must search each element one at a time. You can use the rules of quantum computing to instead run a quantum search algorithm to find the precious gems. Can you find gems faster than your opponent while fighting decoherence and other sources of errors?

Turn order
  1. Your opponent flips one card from their deck. A gem card scores a point and reshuffles the deck.
  2. You either advance the algorithm (increase your probability for the next turn) or measure (find a gem with the probability according to your position). Measuring always returns your quantum state back to the start.
  3. Draw an event: Decoherence blocks your progress, Cosmic Ray forces your next measurement, Extra RAM speeds up the opponent, Blue Screen resets their deck, Quantum Error Correction shields you from the next Decoherence.
  4. First to collect the target number of gems wins.
